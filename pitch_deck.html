The Problem
02 / 09

The Friction of Crypto Gifting & Onboarding

Traditional Web3 transfers require advance wallet knowledge, shutting down spontaneous social gifting and newcomer onboarding.

🛑 The Address Prerequisite Barrier

You cannot send crypto to friends or newcomers without first asking for their public wallet address. This destroys surprise gifts, holiday red envelopes, community rewards, and viral peer-to-peer acquisition.

🏦 Custodial Red Packet Vulnerabilities

Existing tipping bots on Telegram or Discord rely on centralized servers holding custody of private keys and funds. They are prone to server downtime, database leaks, platform bans, and rug pulls.

🤖 Mempool Front-Running & Link Theft

Naive claim links that embed raw private keys or pre-signed transactions are instantly intercepted by automated MEV bots scanning the public mempool, stealing the funds before the recipient can claim.

🔒 Trapped Funds & Dust Fragmentation

Most existing contracts leave fractional dust stranded due to division remainders, or permanently trap funds when a recipient fails to claim their gift within an active window.

The Solution
03 / 09

Sendly: Trustless Gifting with Pure Client Cryptography

Lock BOT on-chain, share a link, and let recipients claim instantly with mathematical front-running immunity.

🔗 Share Via Any Social Link

Send gifts over Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, X, or email as a simple URL fragment. Recipients open the link directly on mobile or desktop without pre-registering an address.

🛡️ Zero-Leak Ephemeral Keys

The ephemeral private key exists strictly in the browser URL fragment (#/claim/...), which HTTP standards guarantee is never transmitted to any web server. Zero backend, zero database.

⚡ EIP-191 Front-Running Proof

The claimer signs computeDigest(packetId, msg.sender) locally. Because the digest binds to the recipient's address, copied signatures are mathematically useless to MEV bots.

✨ 100% Dust-Free Conservation

Each slot receives an equal share, with any division remainder dust automatically granted to the final claimer. Payouts always add up perfectly to 100% of the locked BOT.

⏳ Trustless Expiry & Reclaim

If slots go unclaimed after the expiration timer (1 to 720 hours), the original sender can reclaim every remaining wei with a single click. Funds are never lost or stranded.

🤝 1 to 100 Multi-Claim Slots

Send individual gifts or split packets across groups of up to 100 people. Ideal for team rewards, AMA giveaways, gaming tournaments, and community celebrations.
